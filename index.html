Who we are

Restaurant people,
first and foremost.

Wolverine Management is a restaurant strategies group founded in South Florida. Every concept is treated as its own world—with a distinct cuisine, point of view, atmosphere and reason to return.

The work goes beyond a menu. Wolverine brings together concept development, day-to-day operations, cost discipline, team leadership and experience design to create hospitality businesses that feel intentional from the front door to the final round.
